Why not record the whole game?

You came to watch softball, not manage a two-hour video file.

Full-game recording makes the parent behind the phone do a second job: stay on the camera, keep the frame pointed at the action, remember every big moment, and sort through hours of footage later. That is a lot to ask when the best plays happen without warning.

Sideline Save keeps recent action ready while you watch. When your daughter turns a double play, lines a shot into the gap, makes a diving catch, or rounds third, tap Record after the moment and the app reaches back to save the beginning of it.

Make the post pop

HDR can keep a softball highlight looking like the real field.

Bright sun, white uniforms, deep dugout shadows, and green grass can all show up in the same softball frame. On supported iPhones, Sideline Save can preserve High Dynamic Range video so more of those highlights, shadows, and natural contrast stay in the clip.

That can make a visible difference when you share to a compatible Instagram workflow or watch the clip on an HDR-capable iPhone or TV. If you compare an HDR Sideline Save clip with an SDR GameChanger clip, the Sideline Save version can retain more of the bright and dark detail that makes the play feel alive.

HDR is not a filter and it is not guaranteed on every phone or sharing path. The iPhone, capture format, saved file, display, and social app all matter. Sideline Save reports the clip’s actual HDR or SDR status in its capture metadata.

Common questions

Softball highlights, without the extra work.

Do I have to record the whole softball game?

No. Sideline Save keeps recent action ready while you watch, so you can tap Record after a play and save the highlight instead of recording the entire game.

How does it capture a play after it happens?

The app keeps a rolling buffer of recent action. When you tap Record, it reaches back to include the beginning of the play, so you do not have to predict the exact pitch or moment in advance.

Can I share softball videos to Instagram and TikTok?

Yes. Saved clips are available to share from your iPhone. Compatible Instagram and TikTok workflows may preserve HDR when the iPhone, video file, and viewing path support it.

Do I need to edit the video before sharing?

Not to find the play. Sideline Save saves a short highlight centered on the moment you chose, so you can share it without scrubbing through a full-game recording first. Optional editing is still available when you want it.

Where do my softball videos go?

Sideline Save organizes saved videos by date inside the app. The videos you save also go into the Sideline Save album in Photos, giving clips captured with the app a separate place from the rest of your camera roll.

Will every softball video be HDR?

HDR depends on the iPhone model, active capture format, and output path. When HDR is supported, Sideline Save uses it; otherwise the app saves the clip as SDR and identifies that actual format in the capture metadata.
