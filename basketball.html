Basketball, without the extra footage

Keep the drive, the shot, and the reaction.

A basketball moment often takes shape in the seconds before the basket: a pass finds an opening, a player drives to the hoop, and teammates react to the shot. Sideline Save's Basketball preset starts with 10 seconds of recent action. You can choose a 5-, 10-, or 20-second buffer in Settings, then press Record after the action begins.

Sideline Save does not record the whole game. The camera needs to stay aimed at the action with the app open, and the finished videos stay in your Photos library and Sideline Save's on-device Saved Videos view.

Common questions

How basketball recording works.

Does Sideline Save record the whole basketball game?

No. While the app is open with the camera ready, it keeps only the recent 5, 10, or 20 seconds temporarily available. A video is saved when you tap Record and then stop after the play.

Can I tap Record after the shot starts?

Yes. If Sideline Save was already open and aimed at the play, the selected recent-action buffer can include the moments before you tapped Record.

Where do the finished videos go?

Finished videos are saved locally to your Photos library and appear in Sideline Save's Saved Videos view. Sideline Save does not upload your sports footage to an account.

How much does Sideline Save cost?

Sideline Save is free to download and try with up to 15 successfully saved videos. Sideline Save Pro unlocks unlimited saves through an annual subscription; the current price is shown in the App Store and in the app before purchase.
